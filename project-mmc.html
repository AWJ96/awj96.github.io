Steady‑State Analysis Tool for Modular Multilevel Converters in VSC‑HVDC Systems

Institution: Imperial College London
Supervisor: Dr. Adria Junyent‑Ferre
Tools & Techniques: MATLAB, GUI Development, Converter Modeling, Signal Processing, Power Electronics

This project develops a MATLAB‑based tool for the steady‑state analysis of Modular Multilevel Converters (MMCs) used in Voltage Source Converter–High Voltage Direct Current (VSC‑HVDC) systems. The tool lets users configure converter parameters and immediately visualize key internal behaviors such as arm currents, capacitor‑voltage ripple, submodule energy, and grid‑side waveforms. It also computes the feasible active/reactive power (P–Q) operating region to support controller design, operating‑point selection, and engineering validation.

Key Contributions

  • Designed and implemented a robust, GUI‑based MATLAB application for steady‑state MMC analysis.
  • Modeled internal converter behavior, including submodule capacitance effects, circulating currents, and power exchange between arms and the grid.
  • Enabled parameter sensitivity studies (e.g., AC‑side filter impedance, submodule capacitance, arm inductance) with real‑time feedback.
  • Computed and visualized the converter’s P–Q operating region with clear tagging of voltage‑limited and current‑limited segments.
  • Established a clean interface for future extensions such as transient studies, fault response, and controller‑in‑the‑loop testing.

Methodology

The solver enforces a fixed‑MVA nameplate and evaluates feasibility at each operating angle by checking converter voltage headroom and current limits. After any parameter edit, safe operating ranges are recomputed and the waveforms and P–Q plane update in real time.

  • Limits: With modulation index m=1, the voltage constraints are Vac ≤ m·Vdc·√(3/8) and Vdc ≥ Vac·√(8/3)/m. The fixed‑nameplate constraint is P² + Q² ≤ |S|².
  • Boundary search: For θ in [0°, 360°], the algorithm increases |S| until a voltage or current limit is reached, then refines the boundary point via bisection. Segments are labeled voltage‑limited (magenta) or current‑limited (red).
  • Waveform synthesis: Grid‑side voltages and currents at the PCC include the filter (R, L) dynamics. Arm voltages and currents include the arm inductance derivative terms.
  • Submodule capacitors: Per‑arm energy integration (E ← E + P·Δt) maps to equivalent SM voltage V = √(2E/(C·N)). An optional drift mode injects a small bias to visualize long‑term imbalance.
  • Safe‑zone UI: Every slider shows a live safe band and textual reason (e.g., fixed‑MVA current limit or voltage headroom at the filter), and the error indicator highlights any violation.
